In Firebrat → Conversion settings, enter the matching base address and your private key. Select Load models, choose an available text model, then Test connection. For OpenRouter, openrouter/free routes to available free models. Other model names and free-tier availability can change: check your provider's current catalog and quotas.

Free tier does not mean unlimited usage or guaranteed access. Regional eligibility, rate limits, data policies and model availability apply. Keep billing disabled if you intend to stay on the free tier. Never post your key in comments, screenshots or videos.

Cloud language requests may send text or character information to your chosen provider, depending on the feature. Narration can run locally; fully offline conversion with downloaded models needs no cloud key.
