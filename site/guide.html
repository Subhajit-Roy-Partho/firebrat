Get started

Pick your path: private on your phone, or faster with a free LLM

Firebrat can turn a PDF into an audiobook entirely on your phone, with nothing leaving the device, or use a free cloud LLM key to convert much faster. You choose per book, and you can change your mind any time.

Privacy first

Private, offline reading is the default

Many people care more about privacy than convenience, so the most private option needs no account, no server and no key. The cloud options below are always opt-in.

What stays on your phone

  • Offline mode: import a finished book file (.tar.gz or .zip) and read and listen with no server or account.
  • On-device mode: your phone converts the PDF with a local model and a neural voice. After a one-time model and voice download, nothing leaves the device.
  • Guests can use both. No sign-in is required to read, import or convert on the device.

What leaves your phone, and only if you turn it on

  • Cloud helper sends the hard passages (equations, figures, tables) to the LLM you configured.
  • Use the cloud LLM for every passage sends all of the book’s text to that provider. Leave it off for private books.
  • Research characters online sends only the book title and character names, never the book’s text.
  • Google Drive sync stores your finished books in your own Drive.

Free LLM providers may log prompts. Use the on-device model for anything confidential.

Faster conversion

Use a free LLM instead of the on-device model

A cloud model is far faster than a phone and needs no multi-gigabyte model download. Several services give out free keys that work with Firebrat, because they all speak the same OpenAI-compatible protocol.

Option 1: OpenRouter (recommended)

  1. Create a free account at openrouter.ai.
  2. Open Keys (openrouter.ai/keys) and choose Create key. Copy it now; it is shown once.
  3. Browse openrouter.ai/models, filter by Free, and note a model whose ID ends in :free. You can also let the app list them for you (step 5).
  4. In Firebrat open Menu › Conversion settings › On-device and turn on Allow cloud helper.
  5. Enter https://openrouter.ai/api/v1 as the LLM URL, paste your key, then tap Load models. Pick a free model from the list (type to search).
  6. Tap Test connection. A green “Connected” message means it works. Then tap Save.
  7. For the biggest speed-up, turn on Use the cloud LLM for every passage. No local model is downloaded or loaded.

Free-tier limits: OpenRouter allows 20 requests a minute on free models, and 50 requests a day until you have purchased at least 10 credits, after which the daily limit is 1,000. Long books need many requests, so a free key suits shorter books, or lets you convert a longer book over a few days. Free model lists change often; always check the current list.

Option 2: Groq

Create a key at console.groq.com/keys. LLM URL: https://api.groq.com/openai/v1. Tap Load models to see what your account can use.

Option 3: Google AI Studio (Gemini)

Create a key at aistudio.google.com. LLM URL: https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai/. Google offers a free tier with its own rate limits; check the current limits on their pricing page.

Any other OpenAI-compatible service

OpenAI, nano-gpt, a local server such as Ollama or LM Studio on your network, and many others work the same way: URL, key, model name. The URL usually ends in /v1.

Cloud helper only

Easy passages stay on the phone; only equations, figures and tables go to the cloud. A good balance for private-leaning users.

Cloud for every passage

Fastest. Skips the local model entirely, but all of the book’s text goes to your provider.

Fully local

Cloud helper off. Slowest, most private. Nothing leaves the phone after the one-time downloads.

Books you can convert

PDF, EPUB, MOBI and AZW

Besides PDFs and ZIPs of chapter PDFs, Firebrat converts DRM-free EPUB, MOBI, AZW and AZW3 ebooks, on your phone or on a server. Ebooks are read as text in reading order, using the book’s chapter titles, which makes them a great fit for Novel mode with character voices.

  • Pictures are not extracted from ebooks, and there is no “Source page” button because there is no PDF.
  • DRM-protected books and Kindle files with HUFF/CDIC compression are refused with a clear message. Remove DRM with your own tools, or convert to EPUB first (for example with Calibre).
  • In the app: Conversion settings › On-device (or Server), then Convert a book on the Conversions screen.
  • Queue: on-device conversions run one at a time. Pick several books at once, or keep adding while one converts; Pause keeps everything finished so far and Resume carries on from there, even after you close or update the app; Stop ends the current one and the next starts.
  • Listen while it converts: a book still being made shows in your library with an hourglass and “N of M sections”. Open it to play what is ready; playback continues into new sections as they finish.
  • Keep it running: conversion continues in the background with the screen off. For a long book allow unrestricted battery use for Firebrat (the Conversions screen has a button for it) and keep the phone charging.

Two modes

Novels with character voices, and illustrated books with pictures in sync

Choose the book type in Conversion settings › Book type.

Novel / characters

Keeps the original prose word for word, splits dialogue by speaker, and gives each named character their own delivery. Speakers are recognised from explicit tags such as Alice said or “Hello,” Bob replied. Anything uncertain is read by the narrator.

Beyond male and female, each character can have an age (child, young, adult, elder) and a temperament (calm, cheerful, stern, gruff, gentle, energetic, mysterious). Age and temperament change speed, pitch and expressiveness, and spread characters across the available voices.

Set them yourself in Character voice overrides, one per line:

Alice=child cheerful
Mad Hatter=Michael, elder, gruff
Queen of Hearts=female stern

Names match without regard to case, and a short name such as “Hatter” also matches “Mad Hatter”. Overrides that match nobody are reported in the conversion log, so typos are easy to spot.

Let the web figure it out

Turn on Research characters online. Firebrat finds the book’s Wikipedia article and asks your LLM to describe each character’s age and temperament, then applies it. Only the title and character names are sent, never the book’s text. This needs an LLM URL, key and model (see above) or a server. Your own overrides always win over research, and any failure simply skips the step.

Check who was found

After a novel is converted, open Cast & voices (the people icon in the reader, or long-press the book in your library). It lists every speaker with their number of lines, shows what share of the book’s dialogue was attributed to a named character (the rest is read by the narrator), and shows each character’s age, temperament and voice, plus what the web research found and missed. A character appears only when the book tags their lines (“Elizabeth said”), so the percentage is the honest measure of coverage.

Illustrated / technical

For textbooks, datasheets and papers: figures, tables and formulas stay on screen and highlighted exactly while they are being discussed, and Source page opens the original PDF page.

Backup and sync

Google Drive sync

Keep your converted books safe and move them between devices through your own Google Drive.

  1. Sign in with Google from the menu (Drive sync needs an account; reading and local conversion do not).
  2. Open Conversion settings. Under Get finished books from where? choose My Google Drive, then tap Connect Drive and approve the permission.
  3. New books back up to a Firebrat folder in your Drive, one zip per book. Tap Sync now on another device to pull them, or use Per-book backup to choose which books to upload.
  4. Open Menu › Sync & backup to keep your reading position in each book across devices.

Privacy: Firebrat asks only for access to files it creates itself (the “drive.file” permission). It cannot see or change anything else in your Drive. You can turn sync off at any time.

If something goes wrong

  • “The API key was rejected” (401/403): paste the whole key again and check it has not been revoked.
  • “Nothing at this URL” (404): most providers need the URL to end in /v1, for example https://openrouter.ai/api/v1.
  • “Rate-limiting” (429): free tiers allow only a few requests. Wait a minute, pick another free model, or continue tomorrow.
  • “The model answered with nothing”: some reasoning models spend their budget thinking. Choose a different model.
  • Android closes the app during on-device conversion: pick a smaller model and set GPU acceleration to 0.
