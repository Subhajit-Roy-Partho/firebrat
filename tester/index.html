Become a tester

Help test the Firebrat Android app before it is public. Testing is free.

How it works

  1. Enter the email address of the Google account you use on Google Play below.
  2. We add it to the tester list. This can take up to 1 day for your account to be activated.
  3. Once activated, open the Play Store page and install the app:
    play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.firebrat.firebrat_app

Used only to add you to the Play tester list. It is not shared. Delete requests: royduck@duck.com.

Prefer email? Send your Play account address to royduck@duck.com.
