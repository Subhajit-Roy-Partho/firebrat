Android service demonstrations

Captured on a Pixel 9a on 24 October 2026. These screen recordings show user-started operations in the installed app. They are silent; no microphone audio or personal notifications are included.

Server download and background narration

The user selects a three-page public-domain book from the HTTPS server, downloads and opens it, starts narration, switches to Firebrat’s public privacy page in the browser, and returns to the reader. Android reports the media-playback foreground service while narration is active.

Background local conversion

The user selects the same PDF, starts on-device conversion, switches to the browser, and returns after conversion completes. Cached language-model results can be reused; this is a foreground-service demonstration, not a fresh-inference benchmark. Android reports the data-sync foreground service during the operation.
